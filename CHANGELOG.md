# Changelog

Release notes for The Burg. `/release now` turns the Unreleased section into a dated `## X.Y.Z (YYYY-MM-DD)`
entry for every release, and the GitHub release notes are taken from it.

## 0.2.2 (2026-11-21)

**An easier start.** A first life now starts on Easy, and Easy itself is gentler: quiet first weeks, slower wear and
a one-time hand from the neighbors. Players on an older life get one offer to try a fresh Easy life.

### Changed
- **🌱 Easy is the default for a first life.** On a device with no lives or records, the start screen picks Easy.
  After that it picks the difficulty of the life you started last.
- **🤝 A gentler Easy:** no bad-luck events in the first 8 weeks, stats wear down at half speed (was 60%), a 6-month
  life starts fresh, the net-worth goals are about 10% lower, and once in the first year the neighbors pitch in when
  your cash drops below $0.

### Added
- **🗝️ Try a fresh start on Easy:** players on an older Classic or Hard life who never played Easy see one card on
  Home offering an Easy life. It never deletes a life: with all 5 slots full it says how to replace one.

### Fixed
- Closing a pop-up no longer makes the page jump a moment later, which could leave the button you just reached with
  the keyboard under the bottom bars. After a reload the page now opens at the top.
- The 2-year game length now says "7 with Auto" instead of naming the old ⏩ button.

## 0.2.1 (2026-11-20)

**Pick how hard your life is.** New lives start on Easy, Normal or Hard. Easy is a calm town life with the basics and
friendly events. Normal adds careers, investing, kids, weather and a small business. Hard is the full game, setbacks and
all. Each mode has its own tutorial, Help, records and score targets. Lives you started before this update keep every
feature and show as 🏛️ Classic.

New players can try a short practice life first, and the game opens new parts as you need them. Help is searchable,
the phone's Back button works the way you expect, and you can see trend lines for your stats and money.

Your saves are safer: a damaged save won't blank the game, and nothing deletes a life without asking.
New ⚙️ Settings let you choose sounds, vibration, text size and a night theme.
Plan for later life with pensions, Social Security, a will and long-term care, and run the money side with insurance,
property tax, a moving housing market, wheat prices, cattle and investing. Jobs have ladders and certificates, money
follows Washington's 2027 prices, and each year brings its own weather. Your body ages with your work, and Waitsburg has
30 new town events. Your lives carry over. Back them up first if you like: Menu → 📤 Back up or restore.

  - **Easy:** the core week, school, homes and cars, a partner and pets, trips and Out and about, with 79 friendly

  - **Normal** adds job ladders and interviews, certificates, the 401(k) and investing, kids, the housing market,

  - **Hard** adds layoffs, strikes, insurance choices, collections and bankruptcy, injuries and care for your dad,
    smoke and storm damage, the wheat lease and cattle, and the story challenges.
  - Help, the glossary, tips, badges, the checklist and What's new only show what your mode has. The tutorial is
    17 steps on Easy, 38 on Normal and 41 on Hard.
  - Records are kept per mode, plus 🏛️ Classic for lives from before this update. The end screen, score card and
    life story name the mode.
- **First hour:** a 3-minute practice life on your first start, a "New to games like this?" setup, first-time tips
  for jobs, school and money, "Good first job" tags, and a note on how many weeks your cash lasts without a paycheck.
- **Things open as you go:** week 1 shows four focuses and the basics. The rest opens at the right moment with a

- **Week flow:** "Next week →" right from an event's result, small repeat events can answer themselves during
  skips, and Menu → 👋 Done for now sums up your sitting.
- **Custom rules** (More options → Change the rules): starting money, how fast prices and pay rise, where debt ends
  the game and how often things happen, with plain previews. Custom lives go on their own records shelf. An

- **Help guide:** searchable, with folding sections in the order you need them.
- **Accessibility:** screen readers hear each week's changes, event stories and stat levels (Low, OK, Good, Great);

- Chips on Home for perks that run out, and a calm help sheet the first time you're $1,000 in debt.
- Tap your cash to see last week's pay and bills.
- **⚙️ Settings** (Menu or the title screen): sounds (off to start, made in the game so they work offline), vibration,
  Normal, Large or Larger text, and a night theme that follows your phone (or pick Day or Night). Menu → ✨ Animations

- **Easier on a phone:** swipe left or right to change tabs, pull a pop-up down to close it, and event choices sit at

- **🏡 Homes and insurance:** choose your coverage and deductibles, pay property tax in April and October, and watch

- **🌾 Farm and investing:** sell or store your wheat, buy crop insurance, raise a cattle herd, or buy wine futures at

- **🏘️ 30 Waitsburg events:** a park cleanup, the museum open house, the Fourth of July parade, the trout opener, a

- **🎓 A longer practice tutorial** that shows the new features, and Help sections for Settings, Finding your way,
  your body over a life, and weather and hard years.
- **👶 Toddler moments:** while a kid is 2 to 5, a sick-day pickup, a daycare spot, potty training, story time and a
  first haircut. Most weeks with kids at home, the week's summary adds a short line about them.
- Things to do under 21 at the tasting room and the wine bar, and an all-ages music night.
- Get ready for a windstorm: trim the limb and tie things down to halve the damage.
- **🎯 Life aims** (under More options): All-around, Contentment, Roots, Adventure or Legacy. An aim moves part of
  the net worth score to what you care about. You can change it on the Life tab once a year. "Good weeks" (happy and
  healthy with cash at $0 or more) show on the end screen and the score card.
- A red **💳 Debt** line on Life Score so far and the end screen says how many points debt is costing you and how many
  getting back to $0 would win back. Coming back from deep debt is noted on the end screen, score card and records.

### Changed
- About 60% of jobs are hiring in a given week (more in harvest and ski months, fewer on Hard). Jobs paying
  $6,000 a month or more start 6 weeks after you're hired. The weaker jobs were given better pay or less stress so
  each one is worth picking for someone.
- The Farm kid starts at the folks' place for $400 a month. Starting hints were rewritten from new play results.
- Retiring asks first and shows your weekly money before and after. Retirees can't apply for jobs.
- Challenges show their progress and weeks left. Debt-free starts at -$20,000 and asks for $20,000 saved; the
  family farm is a 2-year game. Story challenges play as Hard lives, and the weekly challenge as a Normal life.
- Stats level off more gently near the top, a new job's happiness boost comes at most twice a year, health has an
  age cap, and visits give less when you're already doing well.
- A trip week pays 75% of overtime and side-hustle money. The same stop is less fun if you go often, and a 4th trip
  in a month feels less special. Road events tied to a town only happen when you drive there. A carpool only saves
  gas on that job, and commute childcare is only for kids under 13.
- In 6-month games the net-worth part of the score starts counting at half the target.
- ⏩ 13 weeks is gone; use Auto. Good news no longer stops a skip, but a low stat or a long rut does.
- The phone's Back button closes pop-ups, steps out of Help and asks before leaving the game. Toasts are shorter and
  sit above the bottom bar, which is slimmer on small phones. No more pull-to-refresh.
- The job board is grouped by what you can apply for, event choices show a small hint, and the Life tab and title
  screen are tidier.
- **Money follows Washington's 2027 prices:** every job pays at least the state minimum wage, nurses, aides, techs and
  mechanics are paid at valley rates, and food, rent, insurance, kids, childcare and tuition cost what they do here.
  Taxes go by bracket so a raise never lowers your take-home, and the state helps low earners with health care,
  childcare and the College Grant.
- Unemployment pays 41% of your last pay, the 401(k) match takes 3 years to vest, net worth counts the 401(k) after
  tax, and retirement draws follow the required-distribution rules. Pensions pay 2% a year.
- Raises from events and contracts top out at 30% over base pay; the ladder is the main way up. Working extra hours
  pays time and a half and wears on you more.
- Tuition paid while you're below $0 goes on a student loan instead of the credit card.
- Businesses pay a fairer return: the woodshop and food truck pay less, the cattle herd costs more, and the wheat
  ground has a farming bill each April.
- **How the Life Score is figured:** the final score is 60% the average of your report cards (every 13 weeks) and 40%
  how your life ends. It weighs your weakest part a little more, so a balanced life does best. Community counts as
  your average over the whole life, net worth can go below zero in debt (student loans count half), and long stretches
  deep in debt cost a few points. Life Score so far says "Just getting started" for the first few weeks. Most scores
  come out a little lower than before.
- Trucking needs a CDL, and the Bluewood lift is a winter job.
- Every kid gets a college send-off, once, in the week they turn 18, and it says whether it's your oldest, middle or
  youngest. The year in review and the Journal's milestones count the big moments: a new job, a partner, a dog, a
  baby, a house, a kid moving out.
- Big town days like Celebration Days happen every year in their month.
- Windstorms come October to March, at most once a year. Sandbagging the Touchet also starts the levee story.
- The cheaper health plan pays its savings ($20 a month) week by week while you keep the job, and light duty pays by
  the week too.
- Traffic school is only offered once every few years.
- Your business tells you each April what it needs from you and each October what it earned, and shows "This season:
  $0" when it's earning nothing.
- The town scene and map change with the seasons, and the coach bubble in the tutorial never covers what you tap.

### Fixed
- Saves are safer: the game asks the phone to protect them, keeps last week's copy of each life, and shows a calm
  screen with your last save if something goes wrong. A new version waits for you to tap 🔄 Update.
- Backup codes and files restore even when pasted with extra words or line breaks, never make twin lives, and keep
  every setting.
- An event left open comes back with the same choices and outcome.
- A damaged save or a thin backup no longer leaves you with a blank screen: broken slots are skipped, missing or
  unknown details (home, car, job, length, mode, date) are filled with safe defaults, and a backup's date is only
  shown when it makes sense.
- One tap can no longer delete a life: "End this life now" shows your score so far and asks first, with "Keep
  playing" as the safe choice (Escape keeps playing), and replacing a life on a full board asks "Replace …'s life?
  This can't be undone" first.
- Lives, best scores, badges and settings in the beta are now saved separately from the live game, so trying the beta
  never touches your live saves.
- A double tap on End Week plays one week, not two.
- Escape now closes any pop-up that has a Cancel or Never mind button.
- Starting a new job ends any unemployment left over from your last layoff, so it can't be saved up for later.
- A wheat lease taken after March skips that year's farming bill, crop insurance and harvest. Selling a lease pays
  for the time left on it, so renewing and then selling right away loses money.
- A life goal whose badge you've already earned can't be pinned for extra points (it shows, but greyed out).
- Buying a vehicle no longer carries the old one's winter tires over.
- Food-truck weekends count as side-hustle work, so truck owners' hustle skill doesn't fade.
- Extra class weeks carry over into your next program when you graduate.
- The Lifetime progress bar and the score's on-track target use the same measure (age 18 to 65).
- The debt warning and the debt limit agree everywhere: Hard mode warns at -$10,000 and names the -$25,000 limit, and
  Relaxed no longer mentions having to leave town.
- The collections fee and the hospital bill now show as lines in the week, so the lines add up to the change in cash.
- The stress of a promotion counts every week, even weeks with no event or when you're away.
- Your age now turns over on your birthday instead of running a year behind.
- A grandkid's starting pickup and an old car's lowest value now rise with prices.
- A friend's job tip shows the pay the way the job board does, and only comes when a better-paying job is open.
- Raises, reviews, contract deals and union steps show the raise you actually get, and say when you're at the top of
  the pay scale.
- The bluegrass jam event only lands on a jam week, and "Gas prices spike" counts remote days and carpools.
- Helping a friend with tuition only offers what you can afford ("Help with books" otherwise), so it never puts you
  below $0.
- The fixer-upper, food truck and levee buttons show today's prices.
- Wine country weekends come April to October, and less often.
- A buddy's engine fix works most of the time; when it doesn't, the shop costs a bit less.
- Better health coverage also halves the emergency dentist.
- With a Bluewood pass, each Get outside week from December to March is a ski day.
- "Feeling run down" comes when health is under 45.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-17)

The first release of The Burg.

### Added
- **A turn-based life sim set in Waitsburg, WA.** Each turn is one week: pick a focus (work extra, rest, show up for
  town, take classes, get outside or a side hustle), pay the bills and drive Highway 12. Four game lengths: 6 months,
  2 years, 5 years and Lifetime (age 18 until the end, with retirement from 62).
- **Home puts the week first:** your stats, a focus grid, and an End Week button that shows your focus. A strip
  above it shows what the last week did (money with events included, and ▲▼ for each stat). Tap it for every
  paycheck, bill and event. ⏩ 4 and ⏩ 13 skip ahead and **⏭ Auto** plays until something needs you.
- **Mix it up:** a focus works at full strength for 4 weeks in a row, then fades, and after 8 weeks you're in a rut.
  Rest tops out in the low 80s. Pushing through low health with overtime is dangerous, and about two years of
  nonstop overtime in a hard job lands you in the hospital.
- **25 jobs in six towns** (Waitsburg, Dayton, Walla Walla, Kennewick, Richland, Pasco). The list shows take-home pay
  after taxes and gas, puts jobs you can apply for first, and compares each with your current job. Locked jobs
  explain the schooling they need. Layoff risk depends on the job.
- **A whole life:** homes to rent or buy, vehicles to upgrade, school (certificate, associate, bachelor's), a 401(k)
  with an employer match, partners, kids, college, and prices that rise 3% a year while pay rises 2.5%.
- **About 30 seasonal local events**, from Celebration Days and wheat harvest to snow on Highway 12, the Touchet
  running high and powder days at Bluewood. Events with one choice close in one tap.
- **Tappable maps:** a Waitsburg town map where you pick your week by place, and a region map of the six towns with
  the rivers, US-12, each town's jobs and your truck driving the commute.
- **A Life Score** from happiness, health, community and net worth (Lifetime counts in 2027 dollars). The ending
  shows how each part adds up, and your best score for each length is kept. Each New Year brings a year in review.
- **Up to 5 saved lives,** each auto-saving to its own slot. The Menu's 💾 Save game saves now or makes a copy as a
  checkpoint, and 📂 Load game switches lives.
- **Help:** ❓ Help & tutorial on the title screen, a **?** in the game header and in the Menu. A 15-section game
  guide, and a 26-step guided practice tutorial that shows you exactly what to tap. Tips for new players, tap any
  stat to see what moves it, and banners suggest a focus when happiness or health gets low.
- **Accessible:** every button is at least 44 × 44 px, text meets WCAG AA contrast, it works with a keyboard and
  screen readers (focus rings, dialogs, spoken week results), and it has a landscape layout for phones.
- **Installable** as an app, and works offline.
