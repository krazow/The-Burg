# Out and about: Waitsburg's real businesses in the game

_Plan, November 18, 2026. Tracked in the to-do list (TODO.md), the
[Out and about milestone](https://github.com/krazow/The-Burg/milestone/2) and issues #48–#54. Builds on #11 (more
local content)._

Today the town map has one "Main Street" pin standing in for the whole downtown, and the Waitsburg jobs are generic
(Main Street diner cook, farm insurance agent). **Out and about** puts the town's real, open businesses into the game:
- They appear as places on the map.
- You can visit them.
- Some are places you can work.
- Their real rhythms, like Ten Ton's bluegrass jam and Simply Sawdust's winter break, become events.

The business list came from research on November 18, 2026: news from 2025–2026 plus the City of Waitsburg business
listing. Each business has a status:
- **confirmed:** recent news shows it operating.
- **city list:** on the City of Waitsburg's undated listing, so the facts need checking before building (#48).

## How it plays

- **Main Street map.** Tapping Main Street on the town map opens a close-up of the downtown blocks, with a pin for
  each business at its real address order. Preston Avenue businesses (Laht Neppur, Whiskey Canyon, Mr. C's) get
  pins on the town map. Each pin opens a card: what it is, when it's open in the game, and what you can do there.
- **Going out.** A new **Out and about** action lets you spend a little money and time at a business, and each
  business has its own small, capped effect. It's a quick action alongside the week's focus, not a replacement for
  it, and you can do a limited number each week. Businesses are only open on their real days and seasons, which
  includes winter breaks, Bar Bacetto's Wed–Sat dinners and Laht Neppur's grill season.
- **21+ places.** Bar Bacetto, Atelier, The Royal Block and Laht Neppur's taproom only show their drink options for
  players 21 and over, the same rule Wine country weekend already uses.
- **Events.** New business events include:
  - Ten Ton's bluegrass jam, every other week.
  - Ten Ton opening on festival days.
  - Grenache Fest at Atelier Waitsburg.
  - A music night at The Royal Block.
  - Laht Neppur's grill opening.
  - Simply Sawdust and Gaudy Gals coming back from their winter breaks.
  - Nancy's spring garden rush.
  - A Mr. C's cookout.

  Existing events move to real places: Feeling run down goes to Waitsburg Clinic, and the inheritance goes through
  Hubbard and Karl Law. All of them pin on the map, like today's events (#8).
- **Jobs.** The two generic Waitsburg jobs become real workplaces:
  - Main Street diner cook becomes a line cook at Whiskey Canyon.
  - Farm insurance agent can stay generic.

  New jobs, with pay in line with the existing table:
  - Barista at Wolfling Coffee
  - Grocery clerk at Waitsburg Grocery
  - Teller at Banner Bank
  - Medical assistant at Waitsburg Clinic (needs school)
  - Reporter at The Times
  - Ag tech at McGregor (needs school)
  - Trades apprentice with a local contractor

  The game's simulation decides the pay.
- **Life systems.** Banner Bank is where savings and loans live, the clinic is where health care happens, the
  grocery is where weekly food money goes, and local trades fix your house.

## Decisions and risks (#48)

1. **Using real names.** Mentioning a real business is generally fine. Showing its name in a game, with prices,
   events and jobs attached, is friendlier with the owner's OK. The recommended default is a short note to each owner
   explaining the feature, with an easy opt-out, and only factual, warm descriptions. A business that says no gets a
   made-up name instead. Ten Ton Coffee is private, so it's portrayed only the way you described it: open on event
   days, and the bluegrass jam.
2. **Facts.** Hours, days and seasons in the game should match reality loosely (the right days and seasons) and
   never promise anything. A "Last checked" date in `data/places.json` helps.
3. **Change.** Small-town businesses open and close. One data file keeps updates to a single edit, and a yearly
   check goes on the to-do list.
4. **Balance.** Going out costs money. The simulation must keep careless play at 60 or below, idle at 50 or below
   and good play at 70 or above.
5. **Fit with version 2.0.** Player NPCs (#39–#44) will hang out at these businesses, so building Out and about
   first gives NPCs real places to be.
